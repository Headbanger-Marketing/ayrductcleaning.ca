Questions About Your Ayr Request

Why should I include return-side connections in the review in my Ayr request?

If ducts run through an unfinished space, ask whether return-side joints and connections there will be inspected. A return leak can draw air from that space into the system, depending on the layout. Photograph accessible sections and ask which findings support any recommended repair, rather than assuming a particular side is responsible.

For duct sanitizing in Ayr, how should I describe the odour and its possible sources?

Record whether the smell is strongest near a register, in one room, or throughout the home, and whether it changes with system operation. Mention tobacco, pets, cooking, or renovation history as possibilities rather than proof. This helps determine whether ductwork merits inspection or whether another source should be considered.

For dryer vent cleaning in Ayr, how should I take a hot-lint smell seriously?

If you notice a hot-lint smell, stop and inspect the dryer and vent according to the appliance guidance; do not assume the vent is the only possible cause. Share when the smell occurs and whether lint is visible at the termination. This information helps distinguish a vent concern from an appliance issue.

What should I check about the HRV core, screens, and drain in Ayr?

Photograph the HRV label and note whether its core, screens, and drain are accessible. Ask which parts are included, how the core will be handled, and whether the drain will be checked for blockage. Different equipment layouts change access and the appropriate cleaning steps; a quiet unit alone does not establish its condition.

How should I set access and timing requirements before work in Ayr?

Identify opening hours, occupied areas, equipment access, and any periods when work cannot take place. Ask how the proposed schedule affects preparation and access to registers or mechanical rooms. These details help the site contact compare a realistic scope without assuming that every business can use the same work window.
